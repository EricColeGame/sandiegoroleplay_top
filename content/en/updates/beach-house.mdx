## Securing Money Printers Inside Your Beach House

Operating money printers inside your estate is the fastest way to recoup your $1,000,000 investment, but player experience highlights that high-value properties constantly attract lockpickers and rival syndicates.

To maximize profits and prevent total asset loss, follow a layered defense strategy.

```
[ Outer Perimeter: Vehicle Obstacles ]
                  │
                  ▼
[ Main Entry: Locked Doors & Decoy Rooms ]
                  │
                  ▼
[ Stairwell Chokepoint: Defensive Crossfire ]
                  │
                  ▼
[ Inner Vault: Money Printers & Heavy Security ]
```

### Best Defensive Setup for Property Owners

* **Avoid Ground-Floor Placement**: Never place printers near ground-level windows where players can spot or glitch-interact with them from outside.
* **Create Decoy Rooms**: Leave empty containers or unpowered equipment in front rooms to mislead fast raiders under time pressure.
* **Reinforce Interior Doors**: Always lock secondary interior doors to force intruders to burn through lockpicks or breaching tools.
* **Maintain Active Guard Rotations**: When running multiple high-output printers, ensure at least one armed faction member covers the main staircase.

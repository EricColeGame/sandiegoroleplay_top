## Complete Guide to Faction Strategies and Progression

To make the most of the **San Diego Roleplay new update**, choosing the right role and mastering its responsibilities is crucial. Each team demands a different mindset and set of mechanical skills.

### Checkpoint Authority Strategy
Running the entry line requires vigilance. As an agent, your main goal is to balance efficiency with thoroughness:

1. **Verify Identification Quickly:** Check civilian paperwork before inspecting cargo to keep traffic moving.
2. **Watch Suspicious Behavior:** Community reports suggest that drivers who hesitate before lanes often carry illegal packages.
3. **Call for Pursuit Backup Early:** Never leave your inspection post unattended; radio marked patrol units if a suspect flees the lane.

### Smuggler and Outlaw Tactical Tips
Attempting to bypass security with contraband creates some of the most thrilling moments in the game:

- **Use Alternate Pathways:** Avoid the main thoroughfare during peak server hours when checkpoint lanes are fully staffed.
- **Maintain Low Profile Speeds:** Driving at the posted speed limit reduces the chance of spontaneous visual inspections.
- **Coordinate with Distractions:** Team up with other players to stage incidents away from your planned transit corridor.

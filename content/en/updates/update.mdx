## Strategic Tips for New and Returning Players

Maximizing your earnings while staying out of jail requires planning. Whether you are running checkpoints or managing deliveries, use these proven gameplay practices:

- **Learn Alternate Byways:** While the primary checkpoint handles the heaviest traffic, backroads and side alleys offer tactical escape routes during high-speed chases.
- **Check Your Inventory Before Queuing:** Double-check your trunk inventory before approaching security booths. Carrying flagged cargo in an open slot guarantees a search.
- **Upgrade Brakes Early:** When customizing your car, prioritize braking force and tire grip over raw top speed to handle sharp urban corners.
- **Communicate Over Radio:** Officers and agents who actively use team chat coordinate tactical roadblocks much faster, shutting down illegal runs effortlessly.

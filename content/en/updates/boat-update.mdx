## Pro Strategies for Smugglers and Law Enforcement

Operating on water requires distinct tactics compared to asphalt driving. Boats have drifting momentum, wider turning radiuses, and zero physical cover when caught in open waters.

### Top Smuggling Tips
* **Hug the Coastline:** Use natural rock formations and inlets to break line-of-sight against armed patrol craft.
* **Travel in Teams:** Solo cargo runs leave you defenseless against .50 Cal gunboats; bring a passenger equipped with rifles.
* **Monitor Scan Blips:** Avoid high-traffic shipping lanes where Coast Guard units frequently loiter.

### Law Enforcement Patrol Tactics
* **Chokepoint Control:** Position your gunboats near river mouths and narrow channels where smugglers must pass.
* **Lead Your Targets:** The mounted .50 Cal has projectile travel time; aim slightly ahead of fast-moving speedboats.
* **Coordinate with Air Units:** Direct helicopter spotters toward offshore radar pings to herd targets into naval ambushes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where do I spawn boats in the San Diego Roleplay boat update?
Boats can be spawned at the designated marine harbors along the coastline. Look for the anchor icon on your map, head to the piers, and interact with the harbormaster terminal to select your vehicle.

### Can civilian players buy the .50 Cal armed boat?
No. The weaponized dinghy equipped with the .50 Caliber machine gun is exclusive to the Coast Guard and authorized Law Enforcement teams. Civilians have access to high-speed motorboats and cargo freighters.

### What is the most profitable boat mission?
The Offshore Contraband Haul currently yields the highest return on investment, offering up to $70,000 per completed run, though it carries a significant risk of being intercepted by naval patrols.

### How does the San Diego Roleplay boat update affect ground border crossings?
The update alleviates land traffic congestion by offering an alternate smuggling and travel route across the water, forcing law enforcement to split their forces between highway checkpoints and coastal waters.

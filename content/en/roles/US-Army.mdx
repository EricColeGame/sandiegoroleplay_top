## Rules of Engagement, Moderation, and Roleplay Etiquette

Maintaining high immersion requires following strict community guidelines. The development team actively enforces fair play policies, meaning zero tolerance for exploits, auto-farming, or disruptive non-roleplay actions.

### Standard Rules of Engagement (ROE)

1.  **Escalation of Force:** Always issue verbal commands before drawing lethal weapons unless fired upon directly.
2.  **No Checkpoint Camping:** Allow players reasonable space to approach inspection zones without pre-firing approaching vehicles.
3.  **Realistic Pursuits:** Do not intentionally pit-maneuver civilian vehicles at extreme speeds unless authorized during active high-threat pursuits.
4.  **Anti-Exploit Compliance:** Any third-party software or automation tools result in permanent server bans.

| Situation | Initial Action | Secondary Action | Escalation Threshold |

## Pro Tips to Excel in the San Diego Roleplay US Army Faction

Community reports and experienced player strategies highlight several ways to maximize your efficiency and reputation on the server:

## Tactical Operations: Conducting Raids and High-Risk Arrests

Raids represent the core gameplay loop for any dedicated **San Diego Roleplay FBI** squad. Smuggling rings frequently fortify safe houses, hidden warehouses, and underground tunnel exits to store contraband before distributing it across the server.

Executing a successful federal operation requires planning, communication, and strict tactical roles.

```
Operation Flow:
1. Intelligence Gathering ➔ 2. Perimeter Containment ➔ 3. Tactical Breach ➔ 4. Room Clearing & Arrests ➔ 5. Contraband Confiscation
```

### Raid Execution Protocol

| Step Phase | Action Required | Recommended Tools | Potential Hazards |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1: Recon** | Identify suspect count and exit routes | Binoculars, Unmarked Cruisers | Ambush by lookout snipers |
| **Phase 2: Lockdown** | Block getaway routes and rear exits | Marked SUVs, Spike Strips | Smugglers fleeing via off-road vehicles |
| **Phase 3: Breach** | Force entrance through primary doors | Breaching Tools, Stun Grenades | Shotgun traps or blind-fire from defenders |
| **Phase 4: Sweep** | Clear corners and secure non-combatants | Ballistic Shields, Flashlights | Crossfire in narrow hallways |
| **Phase 5: Booking** | Cuff suspects and seize illegal cash | Handcuffs, Transport Van | Retaliation attacks from gang reinforcements |

### Essential Tactical Tips for Federal Agents

- **Maintain Cross-Fire Angles:** Never stack directly behind a squadmate in narrow doorways to avoid collateral damage from automatic weapons.
- **Utilize Tactical Voice or Text Calls:** Announce breaches and room clearings clearly to prevent accidental friendly fire during chaotic engagements.
- **Secure the Perimeter First:** Smugglers often post lookouts outside safe houses. Neutralize exterior threats before attempting a building breach.
- **Manage Reload Timings:** When using high-caliber rifles like the MK14, track your magazine count closely. Switch to your sidearm rather than reloading mid-engagement.

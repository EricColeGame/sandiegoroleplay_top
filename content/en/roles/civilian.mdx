## Surviving the City Checkpoint: Legal vs. Illegal Tactics

The core mechanic that sets this game apart is the active checkpoint system. As a **San Diego Roleplay civilian**, you must regularly interact with player-controlled checkpoint authorities. How you approach these stops determines whether you drive away smoothly or end up detained in handcuffs.

### Standard Inspection Protocol (The Legal Route)

If you are running a clean route, passing the checkpoint is straightforward if you follow basic traffic etiquette:

1.  **Slow Down on Approach:** Reduce speed well before entering the checkpoint queue to avoid alarming border guards.
2.  **Follow Agent Instructions:** Pull into the assigned lane and come to a complete stop when signaled.
3.  **Keep Lights and Engine Manageable:** Turn on cabin lights if prompted, and avoid revving your engine aggressively.
4.  **Answer Inquiries Clearly:** Comply with basic questions regarding your destination and cargo.

| Step | Action Required | What Agents Look For | Consequence of Failure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Lane Entry** | Match speed limit, signal lane change | Erratic swerving, queue-jumping | Immediate pull-over order |
| **2. Identity Check** | Comply with documentation requests | Wanted status, suspicious names | Secondary inspection |
| **3. Vehicle Scan** | Remain stationary inside vehicle | Visible weapons, illegal mods | Manual vehicle search |
| **4. Clearance** | Accelerate smoothly upon green light | Premature departures, ramming gates | Immediate spike strip deployment |

### The Art of Smuggling (The High-Risk Route)

For players looking to maximize profit or adrenaline, carrying illicit contraband across the line is a core thrill. Packing items you shouldn't have requires steady nerves and tactical awareness.

*   **Blend In with Traffic:** Never rush the checkpoint when other cars are waiting. Blending into a line of legitimate commuters makes agents less likely to flag you for a full secondary search.
*   **Time the Shift Changes:** Keep an eye on agent activity. When agents are busy inspecting multiple vehicles or processing another detainee, vigilance drops.
*   **Plan Your Evasion Route:** Always know where you will turn if an inspection goes sour. Identify side alleys, highway on-ramps, and off-road breaks before you reach the inspection gate.

## Rules, Fair Play, and Avoiding Moderation

San Diego Roleplay maintains strict enforcement regarding fair play and realistic engagement. Because the game relies heavily on emergent interactions between civilians and law enforcement, maintaining proper roleplay standards ensures the server remains enjoyable for everyone.

When approaching the checkpoint as a driver, you must decide whether to comply fully or risk evading inspection. Checkpoint authorities are equipped with specialized detection tools, custom vehicles, and barricade controls to manage incoming traffic.

### Checkpoint Operations and Procedures

1. **Initial Queue**: Drivers align in marked lanes and bring their vehicles to a complete halt before the stop line.
2. **Document Check**: Agents verify driver credentials and ask baseline clearance questions.
3. **Vehicle Scan / Visual Check**: Officers walk around the chassis to look for suspicious cargo, open warrants, or illegal contraband.
4. **Secondary Search (Optional)**: If suspicions arise, drivers are directed to the secondary inspection bay for a full search.
5. **Gate Clearance**: Approved vehicles receive a green light signal and proceed into the main city grid.

| Inspection Outcome | Required Action | Potential Reward / Consequence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clean Clearance** | Follow traffic lights and proceed normally | Lawful entry, zero heat, civilian pay bonus |
| **Minor Infraction** | Pay secondary fine or complete roadside check | Delay in transit, minor financial penalty |
| **Contraband Detected** | Immediate vehicle exit or high-speed pursuit | Arrest/detainment or pursuit initiation |
| **Checkpoint Breach** | Ramming gates or bypassing inspection line | Max alert level, spike strips deployed, multi-unit pursuit |

The developers maintain a biweekly update cycle. These live updates often introduce new roadway connections, visual overhauls, optimized checkpoint lanes, and fresh building interiors based on community feedback.

### Can civilians legally cross the checkpoint on the San Diego Roleplay map?
Yes. As long as you pull into an active inspection lane, follow the agent's instructions, and carry no illegal goods or active warrants, you will be cleared for entry into the downtown sector without issue.

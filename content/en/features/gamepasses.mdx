## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the best San Diego Roleplay gamepasses for beginners?
For new players, the **Starter Pack** and the **Bag Upgrade** provide the highest immediate value. They offer essential tools, early cash, and extra inventory space for less than 100 Robux each, making early progression much smoother.

### Do vehicle gamepasses give you the cars for free in-game?
In most regional roleplay games, purchasing a vehicle gamepass grants you permission to own and spawn that specific vehicle tier. However, you may still need to purchase the car using in-game currency earned by completing jobs or patrolling. Always check the in-game pass description before buying.

### Can I transfer my San Diego Roleplay gamepasses to another account?
No. All gamepass purchases on the Roblox platform are permanently tied to the individual account that bought them and cannot be traded, transferred, or refunded across accounts.

### Are team gamepasses permanent?
Yes. Once you purchase team-based **San Diego Roleplay gamepasses**, access to that specific faction, its locker room gear, and associated department vehicles remains permanently unlocked on your account across all public and private servers.

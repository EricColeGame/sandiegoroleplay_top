## Method 1: Police Printer Confiscation Raids

For players looking to earn money through lawful gameplay, conducting warrant-based raids as a Police Officer is arguably the most lucrative active **San Diego Roleplay money method**.

Following the Beach Houses update, illegal money printers have become the backbone of the criminal economy. Fortunately for law enforcement, shutting down these operations comes with substantial departmental bounties.

```
[Switch to Police Team] ➔ [Spawn Patrol Cruiser] ➔ [Patrol Apartment Complexes]
         │
         ▼
[Listen for Printer Audio] ➔ [Secure Valid Warrant] ➔ [Force Entry & Confiscate]
```

### Step-by-Step Police Raid Procedure

1. **Equip the Proper Tools**: Switch to the Police team via the main team selection menu. Ensure you have full access to your standard kit, vehicle spawners, and the Police Database system.
2. **Spawn a Fast Cruiser**: Pull a reliable vehicle, such as the Fjord Crownline, from your department spawner to ensure quick transit between hotspots.
3. **Target Apartment Buildings**: Drive straight to high-density residential areas and apartment complexes, as players frequently rent these units to hide illicit machinery.
4. **Locate by Audio Cues**: Turn your game volume up. Walk slowly down the interior hallways and listen closely for the distinct mechanical buzzing hum that active printers emit through apartment doors.
5. **Secure a Legal Warrant**: Community reports and roleplay guidelines emphasize checking the Police Database to obtain a valid warrant before breaching. Raiding without proper protocol can lead to administrative penalties or wasted time.
6. **Breach and Confiscate**: Execute a **Force Entry** on the door. Immediately rush to confiscate all active printers before the property owner can store or despawn them.

Server roleplay rules generally require law enforcement officers to obtain a valid search warrant through the Police Database before using Force Entry on rented apartment doors. Seizing printers without proper cause violates standard server protocols.

When an officer successfully locates and seizes an active printer, community reports indicate a standard bounty payout of approximately $10,000 per confiscated device.

### Is using an auto-clicker allowed with the San Diego Roleplay super money printer?
Running an anti-idle tool or auto-clicker is a widely used community method to prevent the standard 20-minute Roblox disconnection timer, allowing your printers to generate revenue passively while you are away.

## Essential Tips for New FiveM Roleplayers

Stepping into a serious QBCore environment can feel overwhelming if you are accustomed to standard GTA Online lobbies. Adopting the right mindset ensures fun interactions for both you and the players you encounter.

### Top Roleplay Best Practices

- **Create a Flawed, Realistic Character:** Avoid "superhero" tropes. Giving your character unique quirks, realistic financial struggles, or specific job goals creates far more engaging storylines.
- **Value Your Character's Life (NVL):** Treat dangerous situations as if real physical harm exists. Complying during robberies or dangerous encounters creates tense, authentic roleplay.
- **Use Proximity Voice Chat Wisely:** Configure your microphone settings properly in FiveM to avoid background noise and ensure your voice matches your character's physical distance.
- **Stay Updated via Discord:** The **San Diego Roleplay discord** frequently posts script balances and mechanic overhauls. Keeping up with patch notes prevents confusion regarding new job payouts or inventory adjustments.

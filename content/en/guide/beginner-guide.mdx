## Choosing the Best Team: Free vs. Gamepass Roles

When you first join the server, choosing your team sets the tone for your entire gameplay loop. The game features seven distinct factions, but newcomers can access three core teams without spending Robux. 

Each role features dedicated progression tracks where leveling up unlocks better stats, specialized tools, and new responsibilities.

| Team Name | Access Type | Primary Objective | Risk Level |

| **Civilian** | Free | Smuggle goods, break into safes, rob banks | High |
| **Border Patrol** | Free | Screen checkpoint traffic, scan vehicles, stop illegal entry | Medium |

### How to Switch Teams
If your current faction is overpopulated or you want to switch your playstyle, you can change roles at any time. Click the team management icon located in the top-right corner of your interface (positioned between the shopping cart and home icons) to view server population per team and initiate a transfer.

## The Ultimate Smuggling Guide: Black Market Item Stats

For civilians, black market smuggling is the fastest way to turn starting cash into a massive fortune. Near the civilian spawn, you will locate the Goods Market on the right-hand side. Here, you purchase illegal items to transport across the border and sell to the **Smuggled Goods Seller NPC**.

Every black market item carries a specific purchase price, potential payout, and scan detection percentage. Balancing risk versus reward is crucial when managing your starter bankroll.

| Smuggled Item | Purchase Cost | Base Detection Chance | Best Strategy / Risk Profile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Crate of Avocado** | $150 | 10% | Lowest risk; ideal for beginners learning checkpoint routes |
| **Wagyu Beef** | $350 | 20% | Low-to-moderate risk; balanced early-game profit |
| **Fake Designer Sneakers** | $700 | 35% | Moderate risk; solid payout for experienced drivers |
| **Fake Diamond Ring** | $1,000 | 45% | High risk; best run with decoy teammates |
| **Witches Brew** | $500 | 60% | Extreme risk; highly detectable during standard vehicle scans |

### Vehicle Selection for Contraband Runs
After purchasing your cargo, head over to the vehicle summoning terminals located beside the spawn parking lot. Community reports suggest sticking with standard starter vehicles when running low-tier contraband. Starter cars blend into standard checkpoint traffic better than high-profile exotic rides, reducing unwanted scrutiny from human Border Patrol officers.

## How to Cross the Border: Stealth vs. Combat

Getting across the checkpoint with your cargo requires either patience or serious firepower. As you drive down the main highway toward the crossing, your car automatically slows down near the inspection gates, preventing you from ramming through at top speed.

```
[Spawn / Black Market] 
       │
       ▼
 [Summon Vehicle] 
       │
       ▼
 [Highway Drive] ──► [Slowdown Zone]
                           │
       ┌───────────────────┴───────────────────┐
       ▼                                       ▼
 [Stealth Route]                        [Combat Breach]
 - Pull into scan bay                   - Purchase weapons at spawn
 - Pass detection RNG check             - Eliminate Border Patrol players
 - Drive to Smuggled Goods NPC          - Clear barrier & evade Police chase
```

### Approach 1: The Inspection Route (Low-Conflict)
Pull into an open inspection lane and wait for the Border Patrol officer to conduct a scan. 
- **Passing the Scan:** If your item passes the detection roll based on its base percentage, the gate opens, allowing you to drive directly to the buyer.
- **Failing the Scan:** If detection triggers, officers will taser and arrest you, confiscating your goods and respawning your character at the start.

### Approach 2: Armed Breach (High-Conflict)
If the border is heavily locked down or you carry high-detection cargo like Witches Brew, visit the Weapon Shop next to the Black Market before departing:
1. Drive to the checkpoint barrier and dismount immediately.
2. Engage and clear hostile Border Patrol personnel at the gates.
3. Physically push or punch your vehicle past the gate threshold until it clears the barrier zone.
4. Re-enter the vehicle and speed away before responding Police squads arrive.

## How to Make Money Fast as a Civilian

Beyond running contraband, civilians can utilize several criminal mechanics to stack cash quickly.

| Method | Required Equipment | Difficulty | Earnings Potential |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Black Market Runs** | Vehicle, Cargo Cash | Easy–Medium | Steady ($500–$2,500/run) |
| **Residential Burglaries** | Crowbar, Basic Weapon | Medium | Moderate ($1,000–$4,000/safe) |
| **Bank Heists** | Heavy Firearms, Squad | Hard | Maximum ($10,000+ split) |

### Residential Safe Cracking
Explore apartment complexes and residential neighborhoods to locate player-owned residences. Equipped with a crowbar, you can break into interior safes. Always carry extra ammunition, as property owners may return unexpectedly to defend their loot.

### Bank Robberies
Bank heists yield the highest payouts in the game but demand tight coordination. Banks are positioned past the border checkpoint, meaning you must survive both the border crossing and an active pursuit from the Police and SWAT teams. Form a convoy with other players on the server to split law enforcement attention during your getaway.

## How to Make Money Fast as Law Enforcement

Playing on the law enforcement side offers consistent earnings without the constant threat of losing your entire inventory upon death.

| Task | Team | How to Execute | Payout Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vehicle Inspection** | Border Patrol | Scan civilian trunks at crossing gates | Government bounty per bust |
| **Illegal Printer Raids** | Police / SWAT | Check warrants, breach apartments, confiscate printers | Per-printer seized reward |
| **Active Warrant Arrests** | Police / FBI | Track wanted stars, tase and cuff suspects | Bounty payout upon booking |

### Conducting Apartment Raids
One of the most lucrative police activities involves searching residential towers for illegal money printers:
1. Check your police database terminal to see which active players have outstanding warrants.
2. Navigate to the suspect's apartment building. Having an active warrant gives your officer legal clearance to breach the door.
3. Locate all operational money printers inside the unit.
4. Interact with each unit to confiscate it for an immediate cash bonus. Work quickly, as suspects often respawn or rush back to defend their hardware.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the safest item to smuggle in this San Diego Roleplay beginner guide?
The Crate of Avocado is the safest contraband item to start with. It costs only $150 and features a minimal 10% detection chance, making it ideal for learning checkpoint layouts without risking significant funds.

### How do I open safes in civilian homes?
To crack safes inside residential buildings, purchase a crowbar from the supply vendor. Walk up to the safe, equip your crowbar, and interact with the prompt while keeping a firearm ready in case the owner returns.

### Can free players join tactical units like SWAT or BORTAC?
No. SWAT, BORTAC, FBI, and the US Army are premium factions that require specific Robux gamepasses. However, free players can access full progression tracks within the Civilian, Border Patrol, and Police factions.

### How do I know if an apartment has money printers inside?
Check your police interface for civilian players who have generated high bounty levels or active arrest warrants. Wanted players frequently run money printers in their rented apartments to generate passive income.

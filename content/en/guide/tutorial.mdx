## Checkpoint Protocol: Navigating Security as a Civilian

Passing through security checkpoints is the most critical element of the game. If you choose the civilian path, you have two primary playstyles: law-abiding traveler or high-stakes smuggler. 

If you are playing as a regular civilian, compliance is key. Checkpoint agents have the authority to search your trunk, verify your identity, and question your destination.

```
Approach Lane (Speed < 15 MPH) ➔ Stop at Line ➔ Engine Off ➔ Answer Questions ➔ Trunk Search (if requested) ➔ Clearance Granted
```

For those looking for high risk and high reward, packing prohibited items turns every inspection into a test of nerves. If agents notice suspicious behavior, they will order you to secondary inspection, where full physical searches occur.

| Civilian Playstyle | Risk Level | Primary Goal | Recommended Gear |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Commuter / Legal Citizen** | Low | Travel between sectors without disruption | Standard ID, basic legal cargo |
| **Rookie Smuggler** | Medium | Move minor illicit packages past distracted guards | Hidden glovebox items, compact car |
| **High-Risk Runner** | Extreme | Transport high-value black market contraband | Fast getaway vehicle, fake documentation |

### Smuggling Tips for Beginners

- **Blend In with Traffic**: Do not switch lanes erratically or speed toward the gate; agents monitor vehicle behavior closely.
- **Maintain Composure in Chat**: Respond to agent inquiries promptly and use realistic roleplay responses. Long pauses often trigger deep searches.
- **Plan an Escape Route**: If an agent spots contraband, do not hesitate—know your exit route before you pull up to the inspection window.

## Law Enforcement and Checkpoint Agent Operations

Taking on the role of an agent places you in charge of maintaining public safety and controlling the border flow. As an enforcement officer, you operate the inspection lines, run background checks, and inspect suspicious vehicles for hidden contraband.

This section of our **San Diego Roleplay tutorial** breaks down the standard operating procedure for running an efficient checkpoint lane.

| Stage | Action Required | Failure Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Visual Scan** | Check vehicle plates, body damage, and driver behavior | Missing obvious contraband in plain view |
| **Documentation Check** | Verify player identity and permit validity | Allowing unauthorized vehicles through |
| **Physical Inspection** | Use the vehicle search prompt on the trunk and cabin | Unlawful search without probable cause |
| **Secondary Referral** | Escort suspicious drivers to detailed search bays | Letting a flagged suspect flee into the city |

### Handling Pursuits and Violations

When a suspect flees the checkpoint, discipline is vital. Call out vehicle descriptions, license plates, and direction of travel using the in-game team radio. Avoid firing weapons into crowded traffic lanes, as server rules strictly prohibit random deathmatching (RDM).

## Server Rules, Fair Play, and Anti-Exploit Policies

San Diego Roleplay enforces strict moderation policies to maintain an immersive environment. The development team rolls out regular biweekly updates to refine game mechanics and patch vulnerabilities.

To ensure your account stays in good standing, you must follow basic roleplay integrity rules:

- **No Exploiting or Auto-Farming**: Using third-party software, macros, or scripts to automate tasks will result in permanent hardware bans.
- **Value Your Life (FearRP)**: If surrounded or held at gunpoint, you are expected to comply rather than instantly pulling a weapon.
- **Respect Green Zones**: Safe zones such as spawn depots and hospital lobbies are strictly non-combat areas.
- **No Vehicle Deathmatching (VDM)**: Using vehicles as weapons against pedestrians without a valid roleplay reason is strictly punished.

Following these core standards ensures high-quality roleplay for everyone on the server and protects your progress.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the fastest way to earn money in this San Diego Roleplay tutorial?
The most reliable method for beginners is working legal transit and delivery jobs. While smuggling black market goods yields higher single-payout sums, getting caught results in hefty fines and confiscated goods, making consistent legal work more profitable early on.

### How do I join a law enforcement faction?
You can join basic enforcement roles directly through the team selection menu at spawn. However, elite tactical divisions and supervisory ranks typically require joining the group community, passing training sessions, or earning rank promotions through active duty.

### Can checkpoint agents search my car without permission?
In San Diego Roleplay, checkpoint agents have jurisdiction to conduct standard visual checks and ask for identification at border crossings. However, detailed trunk searches generally require probable cause, such as detecting illegal cargo or catching a driver acting suspiciously.

### What should I do if another player breaks server rules?
If you encounter a player using exploits, griefing, or committing RDM, do not retaliate. Capture a video recording or screenshot of the incident, note their username, and submit a ticket through the official community report channels.

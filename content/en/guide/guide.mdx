## Server Rules and Roleplay Etiquette

High-standard roleplay thrives on mutual cooperation and realistic limits. Player experience shows that over 80% of moderation kicks stem from simple rule misunderstandings regarding combat engagement and value-of-life concepts.

### Critical Rule Definitions

- **FearRP (Fear Roleplay)**: You must value your character's life. If two armed individuals point firearms at you while you are empty-handed, drawing a weapon instead of complying violates this rule.
- **FailRP (Fail Roleplay)**: Acting outside realistic human capability, such as driving a standard sports sedan up a 75-degree mountain cliff or walking away uninjured after a 90 mph head-on collision.
- **New Life Rule (NLR)**: When your character dies and respawns at a medical clinic, you forget the events leading directly to your demise and cannot return to that immediate scene for a designated timeframe (usually 10–15 minutes).
- **Vesting & Priority Cooldowns**: Major crimes like bank heists or large-scale store robberies usually require specific active officer counts on duty.

### Scene Etiquette Breakdown

| Situation | Correct Behavior | Common Mistake (Bannable) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pulled over for speeding** | Pull to the right curb, turn off engine, keep hands on wheel | Speeding away immediately without prior IC dialogue |
| **Involved in car crash** | Roleplay injuries via `/me assesses concussion`, await EMS | Repairing car instantly with a tool kit and driving off |
| **Taken hostage** | Comply with reasonable demands, fear for life | Insulting captors while held at gunpoint |
| **Responding to 911 Call** | Drive realistically, clear red lights with caution | Ramming civilian traffic at maximum speed |

## Essential Tactical Strategies for Law Enforcement and Civilians

Whether you are enforcing the law or running an organized civilian crew, mastering positional awareness and communication determines the outcome of dynamic encounters.

```
       [SUSPECT VEHICLE]
              |
       [PRIMARY CRUISER] (Trailing 2 car-lengths)
```

### Law Enforcement Pursuit and Engagement Standards
- **Maintain Rolling Spacing**: In high-speed chases, keep a two-second cushion between patrol units to avoid chain-reaction pileups at intersections.
- **Tactical PIT Maneuvers**: Community reports emphasize that Precision Immobilization Techniques (PIT) must only be attempted at approved speeds (typically under 45 mph) and away from pedestrian-heavy zones.
- **De-escalation First**: Rely on verbal commands and tasers before deploying lethal force. Unjustified lethal engagement remains a leading cause of internal affairs investigations.

### Civilian Evasion and Survival
- **Use Urban Density**: Long straightaways favor high-horsepower highway patrol cruisers. Break line of sight using alleyways and parking structures.
- **Vehicle Maintenance**: Keep your tires and engine condition above 85% at all times; failing parts during an active pursuit can cause immediate engine stall.
